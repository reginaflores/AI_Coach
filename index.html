Welcome to the age of agentic AI.
What is an agent?
An AI you give a goal. It makes a plan, uses your apps to carry it out, and checks in when it needs you.
What is agentic AI?
AI that does the work as well as answering questions. You delegate, and you stay in charge.
Three things an agent needs
What it knows
Context
An about-me file, project folders and meeting transcripts.
What it can reach
Connections
Email, calendar and files, opened up one at a time.
When it shows up
Routines
A morning brief, a weekly review and a school deadline sweep.
A chatbot answers when you ask. An agent keeps working toward your goals, and it needs all three.

Nine strategies that work
Meeting transcripts
File each transcript in a project folder, so AI starts every task already briefed.
Full account access
My AI reaches my personal accounts. It’s powerful and risky, so you build up to it.
About-me file
One page on your roles, family, people, priorities and voice. Reuse it everywhere.
Get interviewed
Have AI ask you one question at a time, then write up what it learned.
Morning brief
A scheduled daily note: today’s calendar, replies you owe and deadlines ahead.
Spoken weekly review
Talk through your week. AI returns wins, open issues and next week’s priorities.
Photo capture
Snap school flyers, forms and tickets. AI turns them into dates and tasks.
Correct it once
When it gets something wrong, have it update your instructions so the fix sticks.
Shared team context
Agree with your personal assistants on one set of files, so everyone works from the same facts.

  1. Use your AI assistant as much as possible. More context means better results.
